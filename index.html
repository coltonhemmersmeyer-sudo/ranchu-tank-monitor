Alert Display & Behavior

Alert preferences

When enabled, an emergency displays a prominent top-of-screen banner with moving red-and-black stripes. Turn this off to keep the emergency listed in the dashboard without the large banner.

When enabled, Low Water, Critical Level, and Emergency warning popups appear when a warning begins and when the website opens with an active warning. Turning this off hides those popups but does not remove the active warning status or safety lock.

These preferences are saved in this browser. When launched as an installed app, active warning popups are suppressed during startup; opening the website in a normal browser tab continues to show active warnings when notifications are enabled.
