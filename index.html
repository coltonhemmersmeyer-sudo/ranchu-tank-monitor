Heating Plan

No heater selected

My recommendation is to start without a heater. Ranchu goldfish are cool-water fish and generally do not need routine aquarium heating. Fort Myers is warm, but the tank's actual water temperature—especially on the coolest winter nights—should decide whether backup heating is necessary.

I would not choose a very small heater just because the climate is warm: 100 watts is marketed for much smaller tanks, and it may be unable to make a meaningful difference in 137 gallons. First log the water temperature through a cool spell. If heating is genuinely needed, size a thermostatically controlled, guarded unit to the measured temperature difference and tank conditions, then verify it with an independent thermometer.

If a heater is added later, its planned position is near steady filter flow—not against the liner, plants, or decorations—and it must remain fully submerged exactly as its manufacturer specifies.

Alert Display & Behavior

Alert preferences

When enabled, an emergency displays a prominent top-of-screen banner with moving red-and-black stripes. Turn this off to keep the emergency listed in the dashboard without the large banner.

When enabled, Low Water, Critical Level, and Emergency warning popups appear when a warning begins and when the website opens with an active warning. Turning this off hides those popups but does not remove the active warning status or safety lock.

These preferences are saved in this browser. When launched as an installed app, active warning popups are suppressed during startup; opening the website in a normal browser tab continues to show active warnings when notifications are enabled.
